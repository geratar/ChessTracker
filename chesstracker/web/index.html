1. Pick your camera

Choose the camera that shows your chessboard.

2. Mark the board

Click the 4 corners of the 64 squares (not the wooden frame), in any order. Drag a red circle to fine-tune it.

Check the straightened board below. a1 must be White's bottom-left square.

3. Ready to play

  1. Set up the pieces in the starting position.
  2. Take your hands off the board.
  3. Press Start tracking, wait for "Ready", then play normally.

Tips: keep the camera steady and high, avoid direct sunlight on the board.
